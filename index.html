Final-year project

2026 – present · In progress

Single-Voxel MRS for Hepatic Fat Fraction Measurement

I’m developing a plan to measure liver fat with magnetic resonance spectroscopy and compare measurements taken while standing and lying supine.

I reviewed PRESS and STEAM localisation and planned the sequence development and spectral analysis.

Technical plan
  • Develop STEAM in C, using a vendor PRESS sequence as a reference.
  • Use jMRUI and AMARES to fit water and fat peaks, correct for T₂ and estimate proton density fat fraction (PDFF).
  • Validate the sequence on the scanner, acquire spectra at multiple echo times and compare paired measurements.

Two-Switch Forward Converter

In a group design project, I worked on a switched-mode power supply from circuit simulation and PCB layout through prototyping and bench measurements.

Our team demonstrated a regulated 10 V DC output under the specified load.

Technical details
  • Worked on the rectifier, switching stage, transformer and LC output filter.
  • Compared open- and closed-loop operation in PLECS and laid out a two-layer PCB in KiCad.
  • Built PWM and gate-drive circuits, wound the transformer and output inductor, and measured switching behaviour and ripple.
  • Designed Type I and Type III voltage-loop compensators and evaluated thermal requirements.
