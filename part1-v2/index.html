Final-year project · In progress

Measuring liver fat with magnetic resonance spectroscopy

I’m planning a single-voxel spectroscopy workflow for comparing liver fat measurements in standing and supine positions. So far, I’ve reviewed PRESS and STEAM localisation and outlined the pulse sequence and spectral analysis.

Technical plan
  • Use a vendor PRESS sequence as a reference for developing STEAM in C.
  • Fit water and fat peaks using jMRUI and AMARES, then apply T₂ correction to estimate proton density fat fraction (PDFF).
  • Validate the sequence on the scanner and compare paired measurements acquired at multiple echo times.
